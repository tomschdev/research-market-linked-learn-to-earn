This document serves to capture the research questions of this research project,
and evolution thereof.

1. in context of the South African dual-speed economy, how can we leverage the properties of blockchain-based systems to build a proof-of-concept solution aimed at addressing local joblessness?
2. how do we assess the implications, positive and negative, of building this
   system on blockchain? what is made possible by building a blockchain based
solution? what are the costs? does adoption/usbaility improve/same/degrade by
using blockchain?
3. assess the system on tps, security, intuitive usability, change management.
4. what are the perceptions from relevant stakeholders?
5. the case for blockchain in systems that coordinate various groups of
   stakeholders comprising of unknown members.
6. I would like to study/investigate/build a blockchain-based system where I
   suspect blockchain offers useful properties which allow coordination of
market linked upskilling - the coordination of this activity is where the
hypothesised value lies, not just in the activity itself i.e., blockchain-based
systems allow trust to create programmability to create incentives (through
value transfer) which creates coordination (which is valuable)
